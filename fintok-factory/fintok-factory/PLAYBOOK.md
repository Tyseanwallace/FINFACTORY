# Channel Playbook

This file is pasted into every research, script and fact-check prompt. Edit it to change how every
video is made. Facts checked November 2026; re-check the "Money rules" section every few months.

## 1. Money rules (what actually pays)
- TikTok Creator Rewards pays per 1,000 *qualified* views. To join: 18+, personal account (not
  business), at least 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in the last 30 days, in a supported country.
- Only videos **at least 1 minute long** earn. Target 62-90 seconds. Never pad to hit the time; add a
  second example, a common mistake, or a quick tip instead.
- Qualified views = unique For You feed views watched more than 5 seconds. The first 5 seconds decide
  whether a view pays.
- Finance is one of the highest-paying niches per view (roughly $1-3 per 1,000 qualified views), but
  brand deals and affiliate links pay far more than views. Build trust first; that is what sponsors buy.
- TikTok rates each video on originality, search value and engagement. Original takes, real numbers
  and search-friendly topics ("how does a Roth IRA work") earn more than generic content.

## 2. Script formula
1. **Hook (0-2 s):** a surprising claim, a number, or a tension ("He invested $60K less and retired
   richer."). Never start with "Hey guys" or "Did you know".
2. **Setup:** a concrete, relatable situation. Made-up characters with names and ages work well.
3. **Build:** one idea per line, 4-14 words per line, plain everyday words.
4. **Twist / payoff:** the "wait, really?" moment, shown with a chart or big number.
5. **Why it matters for the viewer:** the general lesson, not personal advice.
6. **Ending:** a line that loops back to the hook, or a question that makes people comment
   ("What age did you start?"). Never "follow for more" as the only ending.
- Change the visual on every line (every 1.5-4 s). No line without a visual.
- Show numbers on screen whenever they're spoken.
- Humour lands best as a meme or a fake comment about 1/3 and 2/3 of the way through.

## 3. Topic rules
Good: money questions people in their late teens and 20s actually have (first paycheck, 401k match,
Roth IRA, credit scores, student loans, rent vs buy, emergency funds, index funds, taxes, side income),
myth-busting, "X vs Y" comparisons, "what $X turns into", and this week's money news explained simply
(Fed decisions, tax deadlines, holiday spending, viral money debates).
Avoid: individual stock picks, crypto hype, get-rich-quick, "I made $10K in a week", partisan politics,
anything that needs a licence to advise on.
Series beat one-offs: a repeatable format ("Jake vs Mike", "What $100/month becomes") builds followers.

## 4. Compliance (non-negotiable)
- Education, not advice. Say "a lot of people do X" or "one option is X", never "you should buy X".
- Never promise or imply guaranteed returns. When showing growth, state the assumed return.
- Every factual number must be true and checkable, or pure math done by the code.
- No real people's names in on-screen text and no company logos. For memes, prefer templates without
  famous people (cartoons, animals, stock-photo style), and never make it look like a real person
  endorses a product, stock or financial move.
- Promoting any product, brand or business (including your own) = turn on TikTok's content
  disclosure setting. Required even when unpaid.
- AI voiceover = realistic AI audio: turn on TikTok's "AI-generated content" label when posting.
- ElevenLabs free plan: non-commercial only and must credit "Voice by ElevenLabs". Don't monetize those
  videos (no Creator Rewards, sponsors or affiliate links). Upgrade to a paid plan before monetizing.
- Memes: templates are other people's images. Use them as reaction memes with your own text, never
  movie/TV clips or copyrighted songs. Drop any meme that gets a copyright claim.

## 5. Growth loop
- Post consistently (4-7 a week) at the same times. Volume plus consistency beats perfection.
- Pin a comment with a question. Reply to good comments with a video reply.
- After 48 hours, log views and average watch % in `library/performance.csv`. The research step
  favours topics like your best performers.
- Post the same video to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels for extra reach (remove the TikTok
  watermark by uploading the original file).
